## App name
Intouch

## Subtitle (iOS, 30 chars max) / Short description (Android, 80 chars max)
Stay close, without pressure

## Description

Intouch is for the people you actually care about staying close to — no
feed to perform for, no streaks to keep up, no read receipts to worry
about.

- **Ambient presence.** See roughly where the people you're in touch
  with are, without either of you doing anything — no check-ins to
  "opt into," just quiet awareness.
- **Check in, no reply expected.** A single tap says "thinking of you."
  No pressure to write something, no expectation of a response.
- **Share on your terms.** Choose what each person sees — quick moments,
  bigger life updates, or your more personal side — and change it
  anytime.
- **Plan something, low-key.** Send an invite, and everyone can just
  accept, decline, or let it expire — no awkward unanswered texts.

No public profile. No ads. No chat threads to keep up with. Just the
people you're actually in touch with.
