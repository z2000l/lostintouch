# Intouch Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 9, 2026_

Intouch ("we", "our", "the app") is operated by Propersh LLC,
propersh.com ("Propersh", "we"). This policy explains
what data Intouch collects, why, and how it's handled.

## Data we collect

- **Phone number.** Used to identify your account (sign-in via SMS code)
  and to let you add people you're in touch with by phone number.
- **Name.** The display name you enter, shown to people you're in touch
  with.
- **Location (city-level, approximate).** With your device's location
  permission, we capture your approximate location periodically and share
  a city-level, delayed snapshot with people you're already in touch
  with. We do not share precise coordinates, and location is never
  shared with anyone outside your accepted in-touch connections.
- **Photos.** Photos you choose to attach to a post are uploaded and
  shown to the people you've opted to share that content tier with.
- **Content you post.** Post text, check-in messages, and invite details
  you write.
- **Relationship and interaction data.** Who you're in touch with, your
  sharing preferences for each person, check-ins, and invites.

## What we don't do

- No advertising or ad tracking.
- No selling or sharing your data with third parties for marketing.
- No public profiles — content is only visible to people you've
  specifically connected with and opted to share with.
- No read receipts on requests or invites.

## How data is shared

Data is only visible to people you've formed a mutual "in touch"
connection with, and only according to the sharing level (tier) you've
chosen for that person. Location presence is only shared with accepted
in-touch connections, on a delay, and is retained for a limited rolling
window before being deleted.

## Third-party services

- **Supabase** (hosted in the US West region) — our backend database,
  authentication, and file storage provider. See Supabase's privacy
  policy at supabase.com/privacy.
- Twilio - sends the one-time codes used to verify your phone number.

## Data retention & deletion

Most of your data — profile, relationships, posts, check-ins, and
invites — is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Location
presence is the one exception: it's automatically retained for a
rolling 21-day window and then deleted on its own, regardless of
whether you delete your account.

You can permanently delete your account at any time from the app:
**Settings → Delete account**. This immediately and permanently removes
your profile, relationships, posts, check-ins, invites, photos, and
location history. This action can't be undone.

## Children's privacy

Intouch is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age in
your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Changes to this policy

We'll update the "Last updated" date above when this policy changes.
