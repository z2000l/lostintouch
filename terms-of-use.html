How we moderate

Lost in Touch is a closed, mutual-consent network — there are no public profiles or stranger discovery — which limits exposure by design. On top of that:

  • Automatic filtering. Posts, check-in messages, and invite text are screened for known objectionable language before they're ever sent.
  • User reports. Any post can be flagged, and any person you're connected with can be reported, directly from the app.
  • Blocking. Blocking someone removes their content from your feed immediately, ends your connection, and prevents them from contacting you again.
  • Developer review. Every report and block reaches us in real time. We review and act — removing content and/or the responsible account — within 24 hours.

Reporting & blocking

To report a post, use the "Report post" link under any post in your Feed. To block someone you're connected with, use the "Block" button on their entry in your In Touch list. Both ask you for a reason, which is included in what we review.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local law enforcement or emergency services first — reports made in the app reach Lost in Touch's operator, not police.
